## Feature 18: Aquarium & Ant Farm Activity Types

### Aquarium

Canvas-rendered fish tank with 3-6 fish swimming around. Fish wrap-around when
leaving one wall (reappear on opposite side).

**Backend** (`server/generators/aquarium.py`):
- Extends BaseActivity
- Fish state: {id, x, y, vx, vy, size, type, color_idx, facing, phase, speed_mult}
- Movement: advance by velocity each frame, wrap at ±0.1 beyond edges, 5% chance
  of velocity perturbation per frame, vertical sine bobbing for natural motion
- Static decorations: bubbles (rising columns), plants (swaying), rocks — sent only
  in initial_payload, omitted from deltas
- Strategies: tropical_reef, deep_sea, koi_pond, jellyfish_drift, coral_garden
- Titles: AQUARIUM, FISH TANK, MARINE LIFE, REEF CAM, AQUATIC VIEW

**Frontend** (`static/js/components/activities/Aquarium.js`):
- Blue gradient background (darker at bottom) + sand floor
- Fish drawn with canvas paths: ellipse body, triangle tail (oscillates for swimming),
  dorsal fin, eye dot. ctx.translate/rotate/scale for positioning and facing.
- Jellyfish variant (jellyfish_drift strategy): semicircle dome + wavy bezier tentacles,
  semi-transparent
- Decorations: bubble rings rising with wobble, swaying plant stems, rounded rocks
- Hardcoded color palettes per strategy (tropical = bright, deep_sea = bioluminescent,
  koi = warm orange/gold/red, jellyfish = pastels, coral = warm coral tones)

### Ant Farm

Canvas-rendered cross-section of an ant farm with 8-15 ants navigating pre-generated
tunnel networks. Lower update rate (~7fps) since ants move discretely.

**Backend** (`server/generators/ant_farm.py`):
- Extends BaseActivity, update_interval_override = 0.15
- Tunnel system (static graph, generated at init):
  - nodes: {id, x, y, type} — entrance (top), junction, chamber
  - segments: {from, to} — node ID pairs
  - Generation: 1-2 entrances at top → random-walk downward → branch at junctions
    → chamber endpoints. ~15-25 nodes, ~20-35 segments. Tree + cross-links.
- Ant state: {id, segment_idx, t (0-1 progress), direction, speed, carrying, size, type}
  - At nodes: pick random connected segment (exclude previous)
  - carrying: None/food/dirt/egg — toggled at chambers
  - type: worker (common), soldier (larger mandibles), queen (rare, in chambers)
- Strategies: sandy_farm, forest_floor, desert_colony, crystal_caves, volcanic_soil
- Titles: ANT FARM, COLONY VIEW, FORMICARIUM, ANT COLONY, TUNNEL NETWORK

**Frontend** (`static/js/components/activities/AntFarm.js`):
- Background: sky strip at top + 3-4 horizontal dirt color bands
- Tunnels: thick rounded lines for segments, filled circles for chambers, arches at
  entrances
- Ants drawn with canvas paths: 3 circles (head/thorax/abdomen), 6 legs with tripod
  gait animation (client-side sin() oscillation), 2 antennae lines. ctx.rotate to
  face along segment direction. Cargo dot above ant if carrying.
- Soldier variant: larger head with mandible lines. Queen: 1.5x scale, larger abdomen.
- Soil palettes per strategy (sandy = tan/brown, forest = dark, desert = warm beige,
  crystal = blue/purple, volcanic = dark grey/red)
